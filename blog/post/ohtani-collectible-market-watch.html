The Playbook

Staples for liquidity: 2018 Topps Chrome #150 and Chrome Update #HMT32. These are the units of trade - buy dips, sell into spikes. Condition rarity: 2018 Bowman Chrome #1 has a brutal 41% gem rate, meaning PSA 10s are genuinely scarce; that is the long-term hold. Sleepers: 2018 Chronicles Prizm #23 with only 254 PSA 10s, Donruss Optic #176 at 816, and Stadium Club #138 at 974. All three sit well under $500 in PSA 10 with real scarcity math.

Only buy graded PSA/BGS 10s. Raw cards at $2,000 are speculating on a grade, not owning an asset. Catalysts to watch: any 60/60 chase, a third MVP, World Series repeat, and the 2026 WBC in Japan where Ohtani's international market re-prices everything.
